Flow camera-based physiological sensing

A coach that’s actually paying attention.

Flow reads pulse, breathing, blink rate and gaze from a webcam and names what it sees — including “eyes on the page, brain gone.” The interface holds still so the physiology is the only thing moving.

Tokens

Every value below is a named variable. Nothing in the product hardcodes a colour, a step or a duration.

--deep

Vivid blue for the state you want to be in. Live traces, primary action.

--zoned

Arousal falling. Deep_work’s hue with nearly all the life drained out of it.

--spiral

Arousal climbing. The loudest thing here, because it is the one asking for something.

--break

Warm, and away from the blue entirely. Rest is not a failure state.

--indigo · the product itself

The mark, primary buttons, app categories — anything Flow says in its own voice. It never reports a physiological reading, so it can never be mistaken for one.

--paper → --tick

One paper tone and three greys. An evening variant re-points these four and nothing else needs to change.

--none · absent, not zero

Missing data is hatched wherever it appears — never coloured, never interpolated, never drawn as a low value.

Mono is only for machine names

Lowercase and underscored: deep_work, plus token names and evidence strings. Everything else — including every number — is Instrument Sans.

Numbers are tabular, not monospaced

Readings use the same face as the prose with tabular-nums on, so digits keep equal width and a changing value still never shifts the layout.

Motion

Three durations, one curve, and a hard stop for anyone who asks for less.

--dur-1 · 160ms

Anything under the finger: button press, chip select, hover.

--dur-2 · 280ms

Things entering or leaving: an alert, a probe, a panel.

--dur-3 · 450ms

A state change re-colouring the whole screen. Slow enough to read as a transition, not a flicker.

--ease

cubic-bezier(.32,.72,0,1). Fast out, long settle. Never bounce — nothing here is playful.

Reduced motion is not a downgrade

Under prefers-reduced-motion every transition collapses to instant, the state dot stops pulsing, and the breathing pacer stops animating — it becomes a counted cadence with a written phase and a ring that steps once per phase instead of scaling continuously. A tool for dysregulated attention must never be the thing making someone feel worse. Currently: {{ motionState }}.

States

Never a verdict without its evidence — and never colour doing the work alone.

Each state carries a texture as well as a hue — solid, hairline, dense diagonal, dotted, hatched — so the ribbon survives greyscale, projection and colour-blindness. Segments are square; only the ends of a ribbon are round.

Voice

How Flow talks, and the words it will not use.

Observed, never diagnosed

“Your pulse fell and your blink rate collapsed” — not “you were fatigued.” Flow reports what the camera measured and what changed after.

Second person, sentence case

No title case, no exclamation marks, no praise. A coach who is impressed with you every session isn’t paying attention.

Offers, not instructions

“Let’s lengthen the exhale” and a way to decline. Every intervention is refusable in one click, and refusing is never scored.
